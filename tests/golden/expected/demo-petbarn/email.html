The average cat has a lifespan of 15 to 20 years. Advances in veterinary treatments and nutrition alongside a tendency to keep indoor-only cats has contributed to this longer life expectancy.

If your cat is **over the age of seven**, then they are considered a senior cat, when small health changes can represent chronic conditions that benefit from early diagnosis.
Common Signs of Chronic Conditions
**• Changes in litter tray habits or toileting outside the tray **
**• Increased thirst or urination**
**• Weight loss despite a good appetite**
**• Matted fur or dandruff**
**• Reduced jumping or climbing**
Senior Cat Wellness Blood Test
A senior cat wellness blood screening test provides valuable insight into organ function and overall health - often before symptoms are visible. Blood screening can help detect:

**✔ Kidney and liver disease**
**✔ Hormonal imbalances**
**✔ Arthritis and chronic pain**
**✔ Early organ dysfunction**
**✔ Changes that may indicate cancer**

Regular testing allows your vet to track changes over time, adjust treatment plans early, and improve long-term outcomes.
